Gen X Women in The Villages

FB posting rules

All posts should be specifically for Gen X activities and events only.

  1. No advertising of any kind.
  2. No cross posting of events. If you have posted it on another page, you cannot post it on our page.
  3. Events posted on this page will be for Gen X ladies only (exceptions noted).
  4. If you post an event, it cannot have any possibility of the attendees purchasing a product or services.
  5. You are free to collect a fee to cover the cost of your event, such as the prizes at the putting tournament.

When this group was created, the rule was very strict: no advertising. This is a place to connect and find friendships, not another forum for people to post stuff for sale. There have been a couple of gray areas, and it helps when ladies reach out ahead of time, before they post, so it can be considered whether it is appropriate for this group.

This Facebook group was created to foster authentic friendships for women in The Villages who were born in the same timeframe. Events that nurture those relationships are welcome. This is our tribe.
